My Digital Garden

My research interests lie at the intersection of explainable artificial intelligence and cognitive neuroscience, with the aim of decoding both artificial and biological minds. I’m especially fascinated by the challenge of peering inside the black box of large-scale neural networks, not just analyzing their input-output behavior, but understanding the internal representational logic they develop over time.
This is, is probably one of the hardest if not impossible questions of this era, but that doesn’t make it less interesting. What does it mean when a neural network “understands” a concept — and how does that process compare to cognition in the human brain?
